# Vegetable-Based Complete Protein Combinations

**Target Profile:**
- **Weight:** 90 kg
- **Activity Level:** High (2x Gym, 5x Running per week)
- **Estimated Protein Goal:** ~1.6g to 1.8g per kg body weight = **~144g - 162g protein per day**.

To achieve this high protein intake on a vegetable-based diet, volume is key, as is combining legumes (high in lysine, low in methionine) with grains (high in methionine, low in lysine).

Below are three powerful combinations with quantities scaled to help reach this daily target.

---

## Combination 1: The "Power Bowl" (Lentils + Brown Rice)
**Why it works:** Lentils are rich in lysine but lacking in methionine and cysteine. Brown rice is the inverse. Together they form a complete protein similar to meat.

**Suggested Quantity (One large post-workout meal or split into two):**
*   **200g (dry weight) Red or Green Lentils:** ~50g Protein
*   **150g (dry weight) Brown Rice:** ~11g Protein
*   **Add:** Spinach or Broccoli for micronutrients.

**Total Protein:** ~61g
**EAA Profile:** Balanced.

---

## Combination 2: High-Protein Breakfast (Oats + Pumpkin Seeds + Soy Milk)
**Why it works:** Oats are a grain (low lysine). Pumpkin seeds are incredibly high in protein and lysine for a seed. Soy is a complete protein on its own and boosts the liquid base.

**Suggested Quantity:**
*   **120g Rolled Oats:** ~16g Protein
*   **60g Pumpkin Seeds (Pepitas):** ~19g Protein
*   **500ml Soy Milk (Unsweetened):** ~16g Protein

**Total Protein:** ~51g
**EAA Profile:** Excellent; heavy in Leucine (crucial for muscle synthesis).

---

## Combination 3: Mediterranean Wrap (Chickpeas + Sesame/Tahini + Whole Wheat)
**Why it works:** This is essentially Hummus on Bread. Chickpeas (legume) + Sesame seeds (seed) + Wheat (grain) creates a very robust amino acid profile.

**Suggested Quantity (Lunch):**
*   **250g Cooked Chickpeas (approx 1 can drained):** ~18g Protein
*   **40g Tahini (Sesame Paste):** ~7g Protein
*   **4 Slices Whole Wheat Bread (or 2 large Pitas):** ~16g Protein

**Total Protein:** ~41g
**EAA Profile:** Balanced.

---

## Essential Add-on: Omega-3 Fatty Acids
**Why it matters:** Vegetable-based diets are often high in Omega-6 (from grains, soy, and sunflower/sesame oils) but can be dangerously low in Omega-3. A poor ratio can lead to inflammation.

**Daily Recommendation:**
To ensure a balanced fatty acid profile, add **2 tablespoons (approx 20g)** of one of the following to your "High-Protein Breakfast" or "Power Bowl":
*   **Ground Flaxseeds** (Best source of ALA; must be ground for absorption)
*   **Chia Seeds**
*   **Hemp Hearts** (Also adds ~6g extra protein)
*   **Walnuts**

*Note: Consider an Algae Oil supplement for direct EPA/DHA.*

---

## Daily Summary
If you consume all three combinations above in one day:
*   **Total Protein:** ~153g
*   **Suitability:** Hits the target range for a 90kg active male.
